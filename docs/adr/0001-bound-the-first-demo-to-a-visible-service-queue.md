# Bound the first demo to a visible service queue

The capture plan, selected scene, and filming prerequisites below were superseded by [the solo evidence plan](0004-use-existing-footage-for-a-solo-demo.md). The bounded queue format, staffing-only what-ifs, and time budget remain in effect.

The first demo will use a staged service queue filmed from a fixed camera, with visible arrival, waiting, and service areas. The product will support videos meeting an explicit queue format, with users identifying relevant areas, rather than promise arbitrary-scene understanding or work only on one hard-coded clip. This trades breadth and fully automatic setup for a measurable end-to-end demonstration while preserving the longer-term vision of simulators built from videos of physical places.

The selected scene is an event check-in desk where an attendant checks a name and hands over a badge. The baseline has one attendant; the intervention adds a second independent service position fed by the same shared line, avoiding an unobserved downstream service stage.

The first what-if feature supports changes to the number of attendants, including equivalent natural-language requests. Changes to layout, service speed, or customer behavior are outside this initial scope and must be explained as unsupported rather than silently approximated.

The builder has seven hours per day available, with roughly one quarter of available time reserved for verification, the demo video, and submission. Access to filming participants is still unconfirmed. The builder's evening visits to a panipuri stall motivate the problem; its plate collection, seated eating, disposal, and payment stages are not part of the selected check-in model.

The demo does not depend on participation from the stall's owner, staff, or customers. Availability of friends or family for a separate staged experiment remains an open prerequisite.
